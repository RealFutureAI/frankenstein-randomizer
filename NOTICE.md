- The vendored plugin SDK under `src/SDK/` retains its included MIT license.
- MapSense, the map-generation helper, FMMP plugins, and their dependencies retain their original notices. The portable package preserves `mods/FMMP-Experimental-R67/d2rloader/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` and the files in its `licenses/` directory.

The custom curse source is included to make the modification inspectable. The complete base-mod source and the full randomizer tool are not part of this Git repository. Refer to the respective upstream projects for their source and licensing terms.
